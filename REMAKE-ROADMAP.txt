AFTERHOURS — SOCIAL MUSIC WORLD
Playable development build 0.5 · 20 October 2026

The homage preserves walking into rooms, sitting together, conversation,
music creation, performances, collecting and decorating. This is an original
remake in development, not a claim of complete Habbo/Coke Music parity.

STATUS
PLAYABLE: implemented in the solo browser build.
SHARED: implemented through the Node service and portable Worker backend.
PARTIAL: a usable subset exists; the remaining behavior is named.
NEXT: not implemented. Public deployment is not complete.

ROOMS AND AVATARS
PLAYABLE Click paths, held movement, obstacle routing, distance-driven gait,
         interruption, front/back art, sitting, standing, wave and dance.
SHARED   Connected guest avatars, interpolated movement, appearance, posture,
         exclusive seat reservations and reconnect to saved character.
PARTIAL  Six complete outfits; independent face/hair/clothes/accessories and
         complete emote animation are still needed. Remote gait is simpler.
PLAYABLE Four public rooms and up to twelve personal rooms; names, descriptions,
         tags, search, favorites, recent rooms and five lighting moods.
SHARED   Open/private/doorbell access, entry requests, owner kick/ban.
NEXT     Capacity, passwords, builder roles, room size/layout choice, wallpapers,
         floor finishes, beds, pools and swimming.

BUILDING AND OBJECTS
PLAYABLE Fifteen furniture types, inventory ownership, purchasing, category
         search, item previews, moving, dragging, rotating, storing and Undo.
PLAYABLE Two sofa cushions and single chairs, with routed front approaches.
PLAYABLE Blueprint snapshots and restoring owned pieces without duplication.
PARTIAL  Two orientations. Four-facing art, wall placement, surface stacking
         and recolorable collections remain.
PLAYABLE Lamps/candles toggle; water plants; dispense, carry and drink soda;
         roll dice; link room portals; message boards; pet bowls; jukeboxes.
PARTIAL  Portals travel to owned rooms but do not pair arrival anchors.
PARTIAL  Gift wrapping/unwrapping works locally. Wrapped gifts and music discs
         cannot yet transfer through the shared furniture trade.
PARTIAL  Room rules offer enter/say/sit/dice triggers and lights/message/music
         effects. This is local room logic, not a synchronized Wired editor.
NEXT     Rollers, gates, scoreboards, trophies, inscriptions, collaborative
         building and edit conflict resolution.

SOCIAL
PLAYABLE Chat bubbles, per-room history, say/shout/whisper controls and profiles.
SHARED   Nearby speech, whole-room shouting, private recipient-filtered whispers,
         friend requests/accept/decline/remove/follow, real presence.
SHARED   Block and stored player reports; owner room removal and bans.
PARTIAL  Named resident NPCs provide solo company. They are explicitly identified
         separately from connected people.
PARTIAL  Managed guest identities and Google linking/recovery implemented for
         the limited playtest. Live OAuth/CAPTCHA and cross-device acceptance
         tests require provider setup. Legacy browser guests remain separate.
PARTIAL  Room event name/countdown; no global event calendar or invites.
NEXT     Direct-message inbox, groups/clubs, membership/roles, shared group rooms,
         moderation dashboard, report triage, appeals and account deletion.

MUSIC
PLAYABLE Four instruments, 16/32 steps, three synthesized sound packs, tempo,
         per-lane volume/mute/solo, undo, clear, naming, library and WAV export.
PLAYABLE Immutable versions pressed onto local collectible music discs.
PLAYABLE Solo stage with resident reactions, queue and completed-set rewards.
SHARED   Ordered stage queue, common playback timestamp, audience applause,
         one vote per listener and capped durable performance payouts.
PARTIAL  Shared listening uses browser audio scheduling; drift and mobile
         background/resume behavior still need a broader device audit.
PLAYABLE Personal jukebox playlists.
NEXT     Arrangements beyond two bars, authored sample packs, charts, discovery,
         artist pages, disc gifting/trading, collaborative jams and city venues.

ECONOMY AND PROGRESSION
PLAYABLE Daily gifts, eight quests, seven badges, XP/levels, wallet history,
         capped arcade/performance rewards, browser save backup/export/import.
SHARED   Service-owned balances and inventories. Solo imports cannot change
         online money. Trades commit only after both participants confirm;
         changed offers revoke confirmation; cancel/expiry preserve ownership.
PARTIAL  Arcade rewards validate server round timing and daily limits, but
         result scoring remains client-reported. Competitive rewards need
         authoritative simulation before production use.
NEXT     Marketplace, listings, limited drops, seasonal collections, membership
         perks and durable disc/gift transfer.

GAMES AND COMPANIONS
PLAYABLE Tile Rush: 45-second tile control against a resident opponent.
PLAYABLE Snow Jam: movement, aimed snowballs, collision and a five-hit win.
PLAYABLE Pop Quiz: eight music questions, timer, scoring and personal best.
PLAYABLE Tabby adoption, naming, hunger/happiness/energy, care cooldowns,
         feeding, play, training, learned Sit/Spin and following.
PARTIAL  These are original games inspired by the era, not exact historical
         recreations. Shared opponents, match lobbies and leaderboards remain.
NEXT     Pool diving, balance duels, V-ego San, Uncover the Music, MyCoke Coaster,
         Recycler, Sports Challenge, furniture-game hosting and configurable bots.

PLATFORM AND RELEASE
IMPLEMENTED Uno Platform WinUI/XAML shell targeting .NET 10 Browser WebAssembly.
            The existing canvas game runs in Uno WebView2; it is not all XAML.
IMPLEMENTED Portable Worker adapter, D1 migration, revision-based atomic writes,
            origin restrictions and bounded playtest limits.
PARTIAL     Local two-browser presence/chat and restored identity have been
            observed. Unit/database tests cover ownership and server rules.
BLOCKED     Sites publishing helper credential handoff was rejected by automatic
            approval review: this session disables sandbox approval requests.
NEXT        Public release after remaining game and operational acceptance gates.

RELEASE GATES
1. Complete avatar/object art and keyboard/touch/accessibility audit.
2. Exercise shared seating, trade, performance, doors and reconnect on multiple
   browsers/devices under latency and disconnects.
3. Replace the bounded single-world backend snapshot with room-level storage
   and transactional inventory/ledger records; add idempotent command receipts.
4. Recoverable accounts, moderation operations, privacy controls, load test and
   security review before an unrestricted social-world launch.
5. Audit the historical feature inventory by edition against archived clients,
   manuals and footage. Do not call this feature-complete while major rows remain.
